
# 🎬 Home Alone (1990)

## 📖 About the Movie

**Home Alone** is a famous Christmas comedy movie released in *1990* and directed by **Chris Columbus**.

The story is about an eight-year-old boy named **Kevin McCallister**, who is accidentally left alone at home while his family goes on vacation. He must protect his house from two burglars using funny and creative traps.
